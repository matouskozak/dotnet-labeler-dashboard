Confirmation requires a predictor result and matching label event. Manual and dry-run attempts are excluded. Skip-only pre-labeled items are hidden. Unknown results remain included.

No label chosen includes explicit abstentions with or without a fallback label. It does not imply a known below-threshold reason.

Human label edits and bot reapplications come from timeline history. Adjacent add/remove edits by the same person within 5 seconds share one row. This does not confirm a prediction. Daily lines count each item once per day per result and can overlap.
